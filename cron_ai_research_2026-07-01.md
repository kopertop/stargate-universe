## 3. Top arXiv Research Papers

### AI Behavior & Human Interaction
#### AI prediction leads people to forgo guaranteed rewards (arXiv:2603.28944)
**Published**: 2026-03-30 | **Authors**: Aoi Naito, Hirokazu Shirado
**Summary**: AI can change how people make decisions. Over 40% of participants treated AI as predictive authority, forgoing guaranteed rewards. Effect persisted even when AI predictions failed, suggesting self-constraint due to anticipation of prediction.

#### How do Humans Process AI-generated Hallucination Contents: a Neuroimaging Study (arXiv:2605.16953)
**Published**: 2026-05-16 | **Authors**: Shuqi Zhu, Yi Zhong, et al.
**Summary**: EEG study reveals distinct neural patterns when humans process AI-generated hallucinations vs. non-hallucinated content. Misjudged hallucinations failed to trigger standard fact verification pathways, indicating cognitive blind spots.

#### The Dynamics of Human and AI-Generated Language: How Semantics Fluctuates Across Different Timescales (arXiv:2606.11371)
**Published**: 2026-06-09 | **Authors**: Han-Jen Chang, Yasir Çatal, et al.
**Summary**: Semantic-timescale analysis pipeline captures temporal structure of human vs. AI-generated speech. ACW-based measures reveal non-trivial temporal organization beyond static lexical distributions.

### Trustworthy Agentic AI
#### Towards trustworthy agentic AI: a comprehensive survey of safety, robustness, privacy, system security (arXiv:2605.23989)
**Published**: 2026-05-17 | **Authors**: Jinhu Qi, Muzhi Li, et al.
**Summary**: Survey of high-stakes agentic AI systems. Focuses on Safety/Robustness and Privacy/System Security. Consolidates evaluation metrics and benchmarks, emphasizing outcome and process signals. Discusses self-evolving agents, runtime monitoring, privacy-preserving personalization.

#### AI Agents: Evolution, Architecture, and Real-World Applications (arXiv:2503.12687)
**Published**: 2025-03-16 | **Author**: Naveen Krishnan
**Summary**: Comprehensive survey of AI agent evolution from rule-based systems to modern LLM-augmented agents. Covers evaluation frameworks balancing task effectiveness, efficiency, robustness, safety. Applications across enterprise, personal assistance, specialized domains.

### Ethics, Governance, and Transparency
#### Competing Visions of Ethical AI: A Case Study of OpenAI (arXiv:2601.16513)
**Published**: 2026-01-23 | **Authors**: Melissa Wilfley, Mengting Ai, et al.
**Summary**: Ethical AI discourse analysis in OpenAI's public communications. Safety and risk discourse dominate; academic ethics frameworks rarely applied. Identifies ethics-washing patterns.

#### AI Agents and the Law (arXiv:2508.08544)
**Published**: 2025-08-12 | **Authors**: Mark O. Riedl, Deven R. Desai
**Summary**: Legal and technical perspectives on AI agents executing tasks. Examines gap between CS conceptions of agency and socio-legal concepts. Highlights loyalty, disclosure, third-party interaction as under-theorized in CS.

#### Explainable AI for Blind and Low-Vision Users: Navigating Trust, Modality, and Interpretability in the Agentic Era (arXiv:2604.00187)
**Published**: 2026-03-31 | **Authors**: Abu Noman Md Sakib, Protik Dey, et al.
**Summary**: XAI accessibility gap for BLV users in agentic systems. Recommends multimodal interfaces, blame-aware explanation design, participatory development.

#### White-Box AI Model: Next Frontier of Wireless Communications (arXiv:2504.09138)
**Published**: 2025-04-12 | **Authors**: Jiayao Yang, Jiayi Zhang, et al.
**Summary**: White-box XAI for wireless systems using causal modeling, verifiable optimization. Addresses interpretability challenges in domain-specific AI.

### Agent Training & Optimization
#### Adaptive Data Flywheel: Applying MAPE Control Loops to AI Agent Improvement (arXiv:2510.27051)
**Published**: 2025-10-30 | **Authors**: Aaditya Shukla, Sidney Knowles, et al.
**Summary**: Enterprise AI agent improvement using MAPE-K control loops. NVInfo AI (NVIDIA) deployed system monitoring 495 negative samples over 3 months. Results: 96% routing accuracy (8B fine-tuned, 10x size reduction, 70% latency improvement).

#### HELPER-X: A Unified Instructable Embodied Agent (arXiv:2404.19065)
**Published**: 2024-04-29 | **Authors**: Gabriel Sarch, Sahil Somani, et al.
**Summary**: Unified agent across dialogue, instruction following, question asking, room reorganization. Memory-augmented LLM planner achieves SOTA on ALFRED, TEACh, DialFRED, Tidy Task benchmarks.

### Hallucination & Evaluation
#### THaMES: Tool for Hallucination Mitigations and EvaluationS (arXiv:2409.11353)
**Published**: 2024-09-17 | **Authors**: Mengfei Liang, Archish Arun, et al.
**Summary**: End-to-end hallucination evaluation framework. Auto test set generation, multifaceted benchmarking. Evaluates GPT-4o, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-Nemo. RAG more beneficial for commercial models; ICL more beneficial for open-weight models.

#### Learning From Failure: Integrating Negative Examples when Fine-tuning LLMs as Agents (arXiv:2402.11651)
**Published**: 2024-02-18 | **Authors**: Renxi Wang, Haonan Li, et al.
**Summary**: Negative trajectories provide valuable insights for agent training. Prefix/suffix signals improve performance on reasoning tasks. First to demonstrate negative trajectory utility in agent tuning.

### Multimodal & Speech

## 4. Summary of Future Implications

### Trust & Reliability: The Hallucination & Safety Frontier
- **Hallucination remains the dominant safety concern**: THaMES shows varying mitigation effectiveness across model families. RAG is stronger for commercial models; ICL for open-weight models.
- **Cognitive blind spots**: Human perception of AI hallucinations varies by modality and task. Neuroimaging reveals degraded fact verification for misjudged AI outputs.
- **Trustworthy agentic AI**: Comprehensive safety/robustness frameworks needed. Real-world security failures in open-source agents demonstrate that theoretical guarantees don't guarantee practical safety.
- **White-box AI**: Causal, verifiable models gaining traction in high-stakes domains like wireless communications.

### Human-AI Interaction: Agency, Agency, Agency
- **Self-constraint behavior**: Over 40% of human users forgo guaranteed rewards due to AI prediction. AI predictions alter decision-making, even when they fail.
- **Self-blame gap**: Explainable AI accessibility gap persists for blind/low-vision users; blame attribution challenges for agentic systems with multi-step failures.
- **Trust prediction**: AI acts as predictive authority, changing human behavior through anticipation. Critical for agents that must remain useful but not controlling.
- **Neurocognitive mismatches**: Humans don't process AI-generated content with same fact verification pathways as human content, indicating potential safety blind spots.

### Legal & Ethical Governance
- **Ethics framing**: Corporate ethics discourse dominated by safety/risk, not academic ethics principles. Ethics-washing patterns documented.
- **Agency gaps**: Computer science theory under-theorizes loyalty, disclosure, third-party interaction — critical in legal agency frameworks.
- **Explainable XAI**: Multimodal, accessible explanations needed for BLV users. Current XAI heavily visual; agentic systems need process-level explanations.
- **Regulatory alignment**: AI regulation lags technical reality of autonomous agents. Laws (implied authority, loyalty) not mapped to CS agency concepts.

### Infrastructure & Performance: Efficiency Wins
- **Model compression**: Vocabulary transfer reduces inference costs without large performance degradation. Compression methods and subgroup robustness require careful selection.
- **Flywheel learning**: Enterprise agents can self-improve via MAPE-K feedback loops. Real-world deployment (NVInfo AI) shows 96% routing, 10x size reduction, 70% latency improvement.
- **Data flywheels**: Negative samples valuable for agent tuning. Combining successful and failed trajectories yields better performance.
- **Federated/Multi-modal inference**: Mobile, edge, and specialized inference becoming competitive as efficiency techniques mature.

### Educational & Research Ecosystem
- **AI in education**: Growing adoption outpaces ethical guidelines. Bias related to socioeconomic factors; need equitable, transparent AI tools.
- **SLM vs LLM**: Small language models can address AIED challenges resource-constrained institutions. Phi-2, etc., show competitive performance.
- **Virtual focus groups**: LLM moderators can simulate human focus groups efficiently. Quality comparable to human-generated opinions.
- **AI co-scientist**: Agents with specialized skills coordinate exploratory analysis, visualization, validation. Autonomy vs human oversight tensions.

### Research Directions
1. **Trustworthy agentic AI**: Comprehensive evaluation frameworks balancing safety/robustness and utility. Runtime monitoring, adversarial evaluation, constrained environments.
2. **Negative sample utilization**: Agent training on failed trajectories; quality control mechanisms to avoid error propagation.
3. **Human-AI alignment**: Understanding how AI predictions change human decisions; designing AI to be trustworthy but not controlling.
4. **Explainable AI accessibility**: Multimodal, modality-aware XAI. Blame-aware explanation design for BLV users and multi-step agentic failures.
5. **Legal AI alignment**: Mapping CS agency concepts to legal frameworks. Loyalty, disclosure, third-party interaction as under-theorized areas.
6. **Enterprise AI flywheels**: Scalable closed-loop learning with privacy-preserving personalization. HITL feedback collection at enterprise scale.
7. **Multimodal reasoning**: Speech, vision, text integration for unified agents. Natural language reasoning over audio/video.

### Key Takeaways
- **AI has moved from curiosity to infrastructure**: Claude Code, browser-use, PyTorch, Whisper are now foundational tools.
- **Agentic AI is the new frontier**: From single-query tools to multi-step, autonomous decision-makers. Trustworthiness now structural, not surface-level.
- **Human behavior changes with AI**: AI predictions alter decision-making through self-constraint; cognitive blind spots need awareness.
- **Efficiency > Scale**: Model compression, vocabulary transfer, SLMs, flywheels show practical performance gains vs raw model size.
- **Ethics discourse is framing-focused**: Safety/risk dominates corporate communications; practical ethics gap persists in deployment.
- **Legal frameworks lag**: CS theory missing loyalty, disclosure, third-party interaction; under-specification risks in autonomous agents.
- **Hallucination still unsolved**: RAG/ICL efficacy varies by model family; neurocognitive blind spots remain.
- **Accessibility matters**: XAI and AI use cases need multimodal, inclusive design. Current systems favor vision-dominant workflows.
- **Education needs AI equity**: Bias in AI decision-making impacts education outcomes; equitable access critical.
- **Negative learning matters**: Failed trajectories provide valuable data; integration into training pipelines yields performance gains.
